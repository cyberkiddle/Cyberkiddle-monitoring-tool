❓ COMMON QUESTIONS

Q: Which model should I download?
A: Orca Mini 3B for best balance of speed and quality

Q: How long does setup take?
A: 10-15 minutes total (mostly download time)

Q: Do I need internet after setup?
A: No! Everything runs locally

Q: Can I use my GPU?
A: Yes, edit n_gpu_layers in chat.py

Q: How much RAM do I need?
A: 4 GB minimum (8+ GB recommended)

Q: Can I run multiple models?
A: Yes, download multiple and switch with advanced_chat.py

Q: Is it free?
A: Yes! 100% free and open source

Q: Will my data be private?
A: Yes! Everything runs on your computer
