❓ FAQ:

Q: Do I need internet?
A: Only for downloading the model. After that, fully offline!

Q: Which model should I use?
A: Orca Mini 3B - good balance of speed and quality

Q: How much RAM do I need?
A: 4 GB minimum, 8+ GB recommended

Q: Can I use my GPU?
A: Yes! Edit n_gpu_layers in chat.py

Q: How long does setup take?
A: 10-15 minutes (mostly downloading the model)

Q: Is this safe/private?
A: Yes! 100% private - runs only on your computer

Q: Can I use multiple models?
